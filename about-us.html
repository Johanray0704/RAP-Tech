About RAP Technologies

Established in 2002, RAP Technologies began in Chennai and relocated to Puducherry in 2012. The company has developed capabilities in CAD engineering, software customization, engineering application development and international technical outsourcing.

24+ Years of Engineering Heritage

Combining Mechanical Rigor with Custom CAD Software Development

With more than 24 years of experience, RAP Technologies combines engineering knowledge with CAD programming capabilities to deliver customized solutions and reliable engineering support. We partner with engineering companies, manufacturers, construction organizations and CAD software developers worldwide to eliminate bottlenecks and accelerate delivery.

Our journey began in 2002 in Chennai as an engineering technology venture. In 2012, we strategically expanded and relocated our engineering delivery headquarters to Puducherry, India, from where our dedicated engineering teams now serve clients across the USA, Europe, the UK, Australia, the Middle East, and India.

Founder & Managing Director

N.M. Pakutharivu, Managing Director

N.M. Pakutharivu holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering and an MBA in International Business, backed by 33+ years of comprehensive experience in CAD/CAM/CAE, engineering software development, technical consultancy, and corporate engineering training.

He began his distinguished engineering career as an instructor, later advancing to serve as Technical Head at the CADD Centre headquarters in Chennai, where he led technical curricula, training programs, and software consulting operations. In 2002, he founded RAP Technologies with a clear mission: to provide world-class CAD customization and engineering outsourcing services that bridge mechanical design accuracy with software programming automation.
